---
name: Fixed partner cost and contingency
description: Partner fixed costs and customer-facing risk contingency are independent.
---
A fixed partner commitment is a total internal line cost, independent of effort multipliers. Preserve deliberate contingency factors and customer selling price when correcting missing partner costs.

**Why:** The user explained that unfamiliar partners and underspecified RFPs justify significant contingency; that contingency must not be removed just because the supplier commitment is fixed.

**How to apply:** Treat cost override removal as a return to effort-based costing. Do not interpret a fixed commitment as a request to remove selling-price contingency or change shared role defaults. Any additional internal cost reserve is a separate explicit decision.
