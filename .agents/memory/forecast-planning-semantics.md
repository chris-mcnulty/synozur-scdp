---
name: Forecast planning semantics
description: Why bookings, service delivery, actual revenue and expenses must remain distinct in forecasts.
---
Forecasts must not silently use CRM close dates or payment-milestone dates as service delivery dates. They also must not treat unclassified CRM deal values as service-only revenue.

**Why:** The requested capability is a finance/leadership forward service-revenue view, not accounting recognition or cash collection. A commercial close or billing milestone does not establish when consulting services are delivered; CRM amounts can include pass-through reimbursements.

**How to apply:** Keep bookings separate from delivery allocations, surface missing timing, and allow forecast-only assumptions without rewriting source commercial records. Preserve service-only confirmation when changing CRM ingestion. Do not infer currency or substitute missing probability with zero.
