**Subject:** Constellation 2.8: forecast services, review estimates and schedule updates

Constellation 2.8 brings three connected ways to plan and communicate delivery:

- **Revenue Forecast** helps finance and leadership compare committed consulting
  services with probability-weighted HubSpot opportunities. See delivery timing,
  bookings and missing assumptions without changing your CRM or contracts.
- **Historical estimate intelligence** lets eligible estimate users review
  comparable completed projects and effort evidence before committing to a plan.
  Findings explain their limitations and never change your prices automatically.
- **Scheduled status reports** help project managers prepare weekly or monthly
  updates. Review drafts and recipients before delivery by default, or explicitly
  opt into automatic sending. Per-recipient outcomes make failures visible.

Access depends on your role and active organization. Forecasts are planning,
not accounting revenue recognition or cash flow. Historical evidence depends on
recorded baselines and time. Recurring reports need configured AI, email and
background processing; unknown email outcomes block resends to prevent duplicates.

After your administrator confirms availability, open Revenue Forecast from Reports,
the intelligence review on an estimate, or Scheduled status reports in a project's
Status Reports tab. Read the in-app User Guide and version 2.8 release reference
for permissions, setup and limitations.
